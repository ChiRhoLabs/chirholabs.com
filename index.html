Services & Capabilities

Software Engineering

We create custom applications, utilities, automation, and purpose-built software designed to solve emergent problems.

We prioritize efficient resource utilization, reliability, maintainability, and straightforward operation. No SaaS, no feature-creep.

The objective is useful software that performs its intended function without unnecessary features or complexity. The way things used to be.

Web Engineering

We develop website, responsive interfaces, and practical web functionality built around pragmatic requirements.

From new websites to improvements of existing systems, we favor lightweight, standards-based solutions that are understandable and maintainable.

We don't favor unnecessary platforms, bloated dependencies, or manufactured recurring obligations -- unnecessary complexity invites unnessary failure.

Technical Engineering

We adore ivestigation, troubleshooting, prototyping, and systems integration for problems that don't have obvious off-the-shelf solutions. Sometimes, the greater challenges are also the most rewarding to solve.

We evaluate requirements, identify practical approaches, and establish technical feasibility long before committing to implementation. You can't innovate for that which you don't understand.

The solution might involve software, hardware, or a combination of both -- what is important, is that the resulting product delivers on its promises without creating whole new layers of complications.

Products

About Chi Rho Labs

Chi Rho Labs is an independent engineering venture bringing three decades of information system development and electronics engineering experience to solving contemporary technical problems. We develop software, build systems, investigate failures, and engineer solutions around pragmatic real-world requirements.

Every project begins with understanding the problem. What isn't working? What needs to happen? What constraints matter? We examine the requirements before proposing a solution. When there's a viable path forward, we explain the proposed approach, scope of work, expected deliverables, and price. You'll know what you're paying for before work begins. We favor straightforward engineering, maintainable designs, and dependable results. If a simpler solution exists, we'll say so. If your project isn't a good fit for us, we'll tell you that, too.

Our business is built around solving problems, not manufacturing dependencies. We believe good engineering should make life easier, not create new obligations.
